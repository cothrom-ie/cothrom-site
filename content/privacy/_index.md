This page explains what this site uses to run securely and understand traffic. It is not legal advice.

## Analytics

Production pages may load [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/), a performance and page-view beacon. According to Cloudflare, that beacon does **not** use cookies or localStorage for analytics and does not track individual visitors across sites. Metrics help us see whether the site is healthy (loads, visits) — not to build advertising profiles.

Ad blockers and strict browser tracking protection may block the beacon. Traffic that still reaches Cloudflare’s network may also appear in Cloudflare’s edge analytics for the zone.

## Contact form and Turnstile

The [Contact](/contact/) form posts to an automation webhook so messages can be handled. Fields typically include your name, email, and message. Please only send information you are comfortable sharing for that purpose.

When bot protection is enabled, the form uses [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/turnstile/). Turnstile is a security control (not marketing analytics). Cloudflare may set cookies or similar storage that are necessary for that challenge to work.

## LinkedIn

Footer and Contact links to LinkedIn leave this site. LinkedIn’s own privacy terms apply once you are on their service.
