## What it's for

I work with producers, artistic directors, and event organisers to plan and deliver festivals, live events, and other productions.

As part of the production team, I take responsibility for technical planning and delivery, including budgets, suppliers, logistics, and on-site management. This allows producers and artistic directors to concentrate on their own responsibilities, knowing that the technical and production elements are being managed.

## How it works

I typically become involved once the broad concept and artistic direction have been established, remaining engaged throughout pre-production and delivery.

I work closely with producers and artistic directors to understand their priorities, establish responsibilities, and coordinate the people and resources needed for the event.

When challenges arise, I assess the available options, explain their implications, and make recommendations based on what best supports the production. I believe that clear information and practical decisions allow the team to maintain focus, even when circumstances change.

My involvement can extend from early planning through installation, operation, and dismantling, with responsibilities agreed according to the needs of the production.

## Why work with Cothrom

I bring more than 20 years of experience across live entertainment, international touring, corporate events, and complex technical productions.

My background combines hands-on technical expertise with production management, budgeting, and team leadership. I am comfortable working between artistic and technical teams, understanding the priorities of both and helping them work effectively together.

My experience includes managing large corporate productions with Encore Global, touring internationally with Cirque du Soleil, and serving as Production Manager for the Irish Festival of Oulu in Finland.
