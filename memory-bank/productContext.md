# Product Context

## Who it is for

- Artistic directors, producers, festival organisers (Production Management)
- AV / production companies and local facilities (Technical Direction, System Design)
- Artists and creatives needing technical help (Creative Technology)
- Anyone evaluating Dominic / Cothrom for collaboration (Home, Gallery, Contact)

## What success looks like

- Clear distinction between the four services without artificially restricting combinations
- Professional, confident, collaborative tone (no hype, no unsupported claims)
- Strong visual record via Gallery; service pages use relevant photos without repeating the same image everywhere
- Easy contact via form + LinkedIn (no phone/email published)
- Home: greeting, links, 150–250 word biography, future white-paper hooks

## Editorial principles (from docs/pages.md)

- Clear language matched to each audience
- Emphasise problems addressed and value, not bare responsibility lists
- Bio + CV are primary evidence sources
- Photographs reinforce narrative
- Service pages may share themes/examples without copy-pasting the same block
- Site = professional portfolio + contact point

## Constraints

- No invented experience
- No phone/email on Contact
- Prefer non-duplicate image assignment
- Slow, review-heavy delivery with testing between steps
